Clone Runner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Clone Runner is developed by Mmmm Games. This policy explains what data the game handles. Questions and data requests: fourmalgames4@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Your nickname, level progress, coins, gems, cosmetics, settings and advertising choice are stored only on your device. The game has no user accounts and we run no server that receives this data. Deleting the game or clearing its data removes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, with Unity Ads and ironSource as ad networks. These services may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and data about ad views and clicks, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Their use of data is governed by their own policies: Unity Privacy Policy.

On first launch you choose between personalized and non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google shares purchase confirmations with the game so it can deliver what you bought. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Crash and diagnostic data

The Unity engine may send anonymous technical diagnostics, such as crash and performance information.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Retention, deletion and your rights

We hold no personal data on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to retain. To remove local data, clear the game's data or uninstall it. Data held by ad and store providers is kept under their policies; you can use the rights they offer, or contact us and we will help you reach them. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data.

Security

Data exchanged with the ad and store services is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes

We will publish any change to this policy on this page and update the date above.

Satın almalar Google Play tarafından işlenir. Ödeme bilgilerin bize ulaşmaz. Google, aldığın ürünün teslim edilebilmesi için satın alma onayını oyuna iletir. Bkz. Google Gizlilik Politikası.
